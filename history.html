canned beans in tomato sauce: history

the canned baked bean in rich tomato sauce consists of white haricot (navy) beans, steamed inside hermetically sealed tin cans alongside a seasoned, sweetened tomato purée sauce.

in 1886, henry heinz brought samples of American-canned beans in tomato sauce to London, selling them at Fortnum & Mason. initially positioned as a luxury import, local factory production began in Harlesden, England in 1928, turning canned beans in sauce into an essential kitchen cupboard staple.

the canning process

unlike home-cooked legumes, commercial canned beans are rehydrated and sealed raw or blanched inside the tin along with the tomato sauce mixture. the sealed cans enter giant industrial retorts (pressure cookers) where they are heated, cooking the beans directly inside their own tomato sauce.

classic british beans on toast

a traditional staple introduced as a breakfast and lunch fast-food alternative during the 1920s.

  1. toast two thick slices of white bread until golden brown.
  2. empty a 415g tin of navy beans into a saucepan and heat over medium flame for 4 minutes. avoid boiling to keep bean skins intact.
  3. apply a heavy layer of salted butter directly onto the hot toast.
  4. pour the warm beans over the buttered toast. optional: top with sharp cheddar cheese or a dash of worcestershire sauce.

authentic new england slow-cooked Boston baked beans

the 18th-century american method using a dedicated ceramic bean pot.

  1. soak 1 lb of dried navy beans overnight in water, then drain and boil until skins burst.
  2. transfer beans to a ceramic bean pot with 1/2 cup dark molasses, 1/3 cup brown sugar, 1 tsp dry mustard, and 1/4 lb salt pork sliced into lardons.
  3. cover with hot water and bake at 150°C (300°F) for 6 to 8 hours, adding liquid as needed until tender and dark mahogany brown.

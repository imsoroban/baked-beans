quick bean toast recipe

  1. toast two slices of bread.
  2. heat one can of baked beans on the stove for 3–5 minutes.
  3. pour the warm beans directly over the toast and serve.

cheesy bean bake

  1. dump a full can of beans into a small baking dish.
  2. cover with sharp cheddar cheese and a drop of hot sauce.
  3. bake at 180°C until bubbling and golden brown.

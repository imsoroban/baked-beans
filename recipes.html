toast to sauce calculator





beans in tomato sauce on toast

  1. toast bread slices until firm and golden brown.
  2. empty a tin of beans in tomato sauce into a small pan over gentle heat for 3-4 minutes. stir gently to reduce sauce slightly without breaking the beans.
  3. butter the hot toast right to the edges.
  4. pour the heated beans and sauce evenly over the buttered toast slices.

cheesy canned bean bake

  1. pour a whole tin of beans in sauce into an ovenproof dish.
  2. grate sharp cheddar cheese heavily over the top of the sauce.
  3. place under a hot grill for 3 minutes until the cheese melts into the tomato sauce and bubbles.
